# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owner (Admin) and staff of a small Apple-device retail business (warehouse + shop). The owner checks stock, sales and profit per investor; staff receive batches by scanning, move stock, sell fast and record debts. Reports pages are read by the owner (and exported for investors in Phase 2). Usage is daily, task-focused, on desktop primarily.

## Product Purpose

Replace Telegram, paper and Excel with one reliable system that tracks every device by IMEI, its owner (business or investor), its location and payment status. For the reports page specifically: give the owner a trustworthy P&L view (sales, cost, refunds, investor shares, expenses, net profit) at a glance, with breakdowns by category, location and investor.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Apple-inspired light minimalism: generous whitespace, near-white studio surfaces, hairline borders, restrained accent use (one blue for identity/actions, green/red only for positive/negative money semantics).

## Anti-references

- Early-2000s web admin panels: boxed panels with title bars, uppercase tracked labels everywhere, monospace money everywhere, flat primary-color tables, loud multi-color charts.
- SaaS dashboard cliché: single giant hero metric with gradient, icon-on-every-card grids.

## Design Principles

- Data first, chrome second: numbers are the content; frames, labels and color stay quiet.
- One accent, semantic color only where it means something (profit positive, refunds/expenses negative).
- Quiet consistency: same card, control, and table vocabulary across every page.
- Density with breathing room: scannable rows, aligned tabular numerals, clear section rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text; focus-visible states on interactive controls; no motion beyond 150–250 ms state transitions and reduced-motion safe; interfaces in Uzbek, Russian and English via i18n (no hard-coded strings).
