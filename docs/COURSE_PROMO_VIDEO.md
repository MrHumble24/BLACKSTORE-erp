# Udemy promo video: three options

Companion to `COURSE_PROMO_PLAN.md`. This is the trailer at the top of the landing page. Its only job is to make a visitor buy. Pick one option for Udemy; the other two can become social videos later.

Course facts every option may use: 16 lectures, about 5 hours, a real client, a phone and accessories shop ERP, Claude Code as allocator, Opus on backend, Gemini CLI and OpenCode on frontend, three reviewer agents, deploy to a VPS, mistakes kept in, full repository included.

## Shared rules (all three options)

**Format**
- 1920x1080, 16:9, 60 fps to match the lesson footage. Under 90 seconds.
- Udemy shows the promo muted in the hover preview. The video must make sense with no sound.

**Captions**
- Burn in every spoken line. Max 2 lines on screen, 5 to 7 words per caption.
- Bottom third, inside the safe area. The key word of each caption in the accent colour.

**Look**
- Dark background and the neon accent colour taken from the course cover. One bold sans font for all text.
- Same hoodie and same framing as the cover for every on-camera shot.

**Motion rules (the fast, "hyper motion" feel)**
- Fast sections: a cut every 1 to 2.5 seconds. Never hold a screen recording longer than 3 seconds.
- Speed ramp on screen recordings: play at 8x, drop to 1x on the moment that matters.
- Punch-in zoom to 110 to 130 percent on the line you are talking about.
- Highlight box that draws itself around the code line, bug line or button.
- Big text slams in on the beat: 6 to 8 frames, ease-out, tiny shake on landing.
- Whip or glitch transitions only between sections, 5 or 6 in the whole video. Hard cuts everywhere else.
- End card holds for 3 seconds.

**Sound**
- One music track with a clear beat, 100 to 120 bpm, from the CapCut library with commercial use allowed. Lower it under the voice.
- Whoosh on section transitions, click on text hits, one low hit on the biggest reveal.
- Voice with the same microphone as the lessons.

**Never in the promo**
- Price, discount, coupon or any link.
- Passwords, `.env` files, phone numbers, serial numbers, the client's shop name.
- Income promises or student numbers.

**Who does what**
- You: film the on-camera lines, record the voice lines, pick the clips, assemble in CapCut.
- Claude: build every animated piece as a ready MP4 clip (HyperFrames), find clip timestamps from the lesson transcripts, review the final cut.

## Steps after you pick

1. You film the on-camera lines and record the voice lines in one sitting with the wrap-up lecture.
2. Claude finds the clip timestamps from the transcripts and builds the animated pieces.
3. You assemble in CapCut: voice first, then clips on the voice, then animations, captions, music, sound effects.
4. Check with the sound off. If the first 5 seconds do not explain the course, fix the captions.
5. Export to `~/Desktop/Cap_Cut_upload/promo.mov`. Claude uploads it to the landing page.
