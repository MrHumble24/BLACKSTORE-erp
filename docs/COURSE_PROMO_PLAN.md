# Promo plan: BLACKSTORE Udemy course

Companion to `COURSE_OUTLINE.md`. Two jobs: the promo video on the Udemy page, and short videos that bring people to it.

## 1. Udemy promo video (the trailer on the landing page)

Full production plan with three options, scripts and animations: `COURSE_PROMO_VIDEO.md`. The table below is the first short draft.

Length 60 to 90 seconds. Vertical no, 16:9 yes. Udemy plays it muted in the preview, so captions on every line.

| Time | What is on screen | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 s | The finished ERP: dashboard, a sale, the printed receipt, the phone app, fast cuts | "This is a real shop system, running in a real shop. I didn't write most of it." |
| 5 to 15 s | Claude Code screen with four agent sessions side by side | "Four AI agents built it. Claude planned and handed out tasks, Opus did the backend, Gemini and OpenCode did the frontend, reviewer agents caught the bugs." |
| 15 to 30 s | Client call recording, then the PRD file | "It started with a recorded call with the client. The AI listened and wrote the spec. Then we built it feature by feature." |
| 30 to 50 s | Three fast moments from the lessons: a reviewer catching a money bug, the deploy to the VPS, the first full sale | "You'll see everything, including the mistakes: bugs the reviewers caught, agents that got stuck, a reports page the client hated." |
| 50 to 70 s | Curriculum scroll, the repo tree, the handoff files | "By the end you'll know how to run AI agents like a team: rules, handoffs, reviews, worktrees, deploys." |
| 70 to 90 s | You on camera, course cover | "If you want to ship client projects with AI instead of just chatting with it, this course is for you. See you inside." |

Record the "you on camera" parts tomorrow with the wrap-up video, same setup, same hoodie as the cover. Everything else is cut from the existing lesson footage.

## 2. Who we target, and what each group needs to hear

| Audience | Their pain | The line that sells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freelance and agency devs | Client projects take too long, margins are thin | "One developer, one client, four agents, ERP delivered" |
| Devs already using Claude Code or Cursor | They use one agent, no process, results are messy | "Stop chatting with AI. Run it like a team with rules, reviews and handoffs" |
| Junior and mid devs | Never ran a full project from call to deploy | "Watch a real project from the client call to production, nothing skipped" |
| Small business owners who code a bit | Want their own system, can't afford an agency | "This is how a real shop got its own ERP for the price of AI subscriptions" |

Languages: English first. The Uzbek and Russian versions of the same clips can go to Telegram and local YouTube, that audience is close to you and has little competition.

## 6. Before anything is posted

- Blur the .env password, backup passphrase, MacBook serial, staff password and the real phone number. They are on screen in Lessons 9, 11 and 12.
- Ask the client if the shop name can be shown. If not, blur it too.
- Fix the auto-captions: "Cloud" to Claude Code, "Jim and I" to Gemini, "open quote" to OpenCode, "PRT" to PRD.
- One Udemy coupon per platform: YT, TT, IG, LI, TG. Udemy shows sales per coupon, that is the whole tracking system.
